Arke is portable now: settings, folder views, workspaces, search history, the shelf, the search index, the log, downloaded updates and trusted host keys all live in a `data` folder next to the exe instead of `%APPDATA%\Arke` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Arke`. Copy the exe anywhere and it keeps its own files with it; uninstalling removes the `data` folder too (and the old AppData folders, unless you keep your settings).
